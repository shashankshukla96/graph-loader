# E09 — Prove service behavior and ordering limits

Plan review: **approved**. Implementation: **in progress; E09.F1.S7 and S1 complete; S2–S5 and F2.S1 planned (plan reset 2026-10-31, D08)**.

**Outcome.** Installed-artifact integration evidence for the supported modes and the exact promised guarantees.

**Scope.** Use throwaway services/scratch databases and the locally identified candidate images. The loaded remote IMDb graph is never a test fixture to reset.

**Prerequisites.** E08

**Exit gate.** Every supported mode and ordering case has an executed result with graph/offset/cleanup evidence; unsupported cases are explicitly documented and excluded from guarantees.

Epic prerequisites apply to every story, in addition to the story-specific dependencies below. Estimates are rough focused engineering effort, not elapsed-time commitments. Story completion requires its acceptance criteria, validation, and evidence; implementing a file alone is insufficient.

### Story E09.F1.S7 — Provision the live installed-service proof harness

- Status: complete; independently accepted 2026-10-31 on the shared native Docker Engine substrate (see evidence/E09.F1.S7.md); estimate: 2 engineering day(s).
- Checklist: V6, D5.

- Change surface: tests/installed_service/ live harness (owned Neo4j/Kafka stack, installed CLI runner, observers, query-log lock-wait detector); scripts/service_lane.py and the service-harness lane in scripts/verify_installed.py.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. Give every ordering-matrix.md row O1–O8 (restart, revocation/rebalance, LRU eviction, cross-topic and cross-partition offsets, replicas, manual replay, topic recreation) an asserted supported outcome or an approved D02 exclusion with its counterexample or precondition; no unqualified exactly-once or restart-safe claim.
